Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for a workshop?

Navigate to the Events page, view the workshop details, and click the "Register for Event" button.


Can I update my student department after registration?

Yes, you can modify your academic details anytime by logging in and visiting your Profile page.


What should I do if I forget my account password?

Click on the "Forgot Password?" link on the Login page. Enter your registered student email address to receive a secure password reset link.


How long does admin approval take for new student accounts?

Student registration verification usually takes between 24 to 48 hours. Once approved by the administration team, you will receive confirmation and full access to your student dashboard.


Are campus workshops and events free to attend?

Most campus workshops, hackathons, and guest lectures hosted on StudentHub are completely free for registered students unless specifically stated in the event details.


Can I cancel my event registration if I cannot attend?

Yes. Go to your Dashboard, locate the event under your Registered Events list, and click the "Cancel Request" button to free up the seat for another student.


Will I receive a participation certificate after attending workshops?

Yes! Digital certificates of participation are generated automatically for attendees within 3 business days following the event and can be downloaded from your student profile.
